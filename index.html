ABOUT OUR CLUB

From a Small Beginning to a Growing Community

MURGABANI 1 TO 100 CLUB was founded in 2013 by six young friends with a dream of creating something meaningful for their community.

What began with a small Saraswati Puja has gradually grown into a community united by culture, friendship, responsibility and service.
